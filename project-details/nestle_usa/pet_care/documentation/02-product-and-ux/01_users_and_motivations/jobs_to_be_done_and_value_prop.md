## 2. Value proposition

> **For pet owners who can't express their pet's needs in a rigid form, the Grounded Nutrition Intake & Coaching system turns messy free text into a complete profile and explains the recommended plan in warm, accurate language — so you can act with confidence, knowing an LLM never chose the diet.**

| Element | Detail |

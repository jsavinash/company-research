## Role-by-role (with rationale)

### 1. Associate AI Engineer — Advanced Tax Solutions (MyTAXPrepOffice)
**Sep 2016 – Apr 2017 · 8 mos**

First role out of the gate. Built a document-AI system that reads tax forms and scores its own confidence on every field. A strong *entry-level* AI title that signals you started in applied ML, not general software.

*Why this title:* "Associate" sets the baseline; the work (OCR + sequence labeling + calibration) is real AI engineering, so it reads as a genuine AI entry point.

### 2. AI Engineer — NalaGenetics
**May 2017 – Jul 2018 · 1 yr 3 mos**

Built a variant-triage system that ranks gene variants for clinical review with evidence codes and confidence. A clear step up from "Associate" to a full **AI Engineer** owning a domain (genomics).

*Why this title:* broadened from documents to genomics; "AI Engineer" shows you own AI systems end-to-end.

### 3. Machine Learning Engineer — T-Systems ICT India
**Dec 2018 – Aug 2019 · 9 mos**

Built a real-time e-commerce search and recommendation platform (two-tower retrieval + LambdaMART ranking) on Kubernetes. A move into **ML engineering at scale** (serving, MLOps, latency).

*Why this title:* "Machine Learning Engineer" signals production ML + MLOps — a step up in scale and ownership.

### 4. Senior AI Engineer — Siemens Healthineers India
**Aug 2019 – Mar 2021 · 1 yr 8 mos**

Built a chest X-ray triage and calibrated CAD system with per-site calibration and regulatory hardening. First **Senior** title — you now own a safety-critical, regulated AI product.

*Why this title:* "Senior" reflects ownership of a clinical-grade system with calibration, reader studies, and regulatory (MDR/FDA) work. "AI Engineer" (not "ML Engineer") signals the broader scope: imaging AI, not just tabular ML.

### 5. Senior Data Scientist — Siemens Financial Services
**Apr 2021 – Mar 2022 · 1 yr**

Built a credit-risk early-warning system using machine-usage telemetry (not just financials) with survival models and governance. A **Senior Data Scientist** role — broadening from imaging into financial risk and causal/ statistical modeling.

*Why this title:* "Data Scientist" reflects the heavier statistics/causal/survival emphasis (credit risk), while "Senior" maintains level. It adds variety without breaking the progression.

### 6. Lead AI Engineer — Nestlé USA (Demand Sensing)
**Mar 2022 – Jun 2024 · 2 yrs 4 mos**

Led a demand-sensing platform with GLP-1-aware forecasting, causal attribution, and a conversational copilot. First **Lead** title — you now guide a team/architecture, not just build.

*Why this title:* "Lead" reflects technical leadership (architecture, mentoring, cross-team) on a large enterprise platform.

### 7. Principal AI Engineer — Nestlé USA (PetCare GenAI)
**Jul 2024 – Nov 2026 · 2 yrs 5 mos**

Built the GenAI layer for Purina — schema-constrained intake, grounded RAG coaching, red-team evaluation. A **Principal**-level title: org-wide technical authority on GenAI/LLM.

*Why this title:* "Principal" signals the highest individual-contributor technical level — setting AI/LLM direction, safety, and evaluation standards.

### 8. AI Solutions Architect — Capgemini Engineering
**Mar 2025 – Present**

Designs enterprise agentic AI platforms (hybrid cloud, AI gateways, permission-aware RAG, MCP/A2A, governance). The **Architect** capstone — from building systems to designing the architecture others build on.

*Why this title:* "AI Solutions Architect" is the natural apex — you now define the blueprint, not just implement it.

## Why this reads as progressive

- **Level rises every 1–2 roles:** Associate AI Engineer → AI Engineer → Machine Learning Engineer → Senior AI Engineer → Senior Data Scientist → Lead AI Engineer → Principal AI Engineer → AI Solutions Architect.
- **No title repeats** — each step is a distinct title, so it doesn't look like you stayed in the same role.
- **Scope widens:** documents → genomics → e-commerce → imaging → finance → demand sensing → GenAI → enterprise architecture.
- **Each move adds a new domain** while keeping the AI/ML core, so it reads as deliberate growth, not job-hopping.
- **The last three titles (Lead → Principal → Architect)** show a clear transition from hands-on builder to technical leader to architect.

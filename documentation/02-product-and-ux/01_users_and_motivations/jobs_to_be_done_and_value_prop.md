## 2. Value proposition

> **For demand planners who can't trust history-based forecasts in a GLP-1 world, the Scenario Copilot is a decision-support platform that turns structured and unstructured data into calibrated, causally-attributed forecasts — so you can answer "what next, and why" in seconds, not days, with numbers you can defend.**
